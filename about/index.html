02The studio

Small on purpose.

The studio stays deliberately small. No account managers, no junior handoffs, no telephone game between the person who sold the project and the person doing the work.

ZiegfridDesigner & builder

One person.
Fewer handoffs.

The businesses we work with rarely fit a theme. They have a particular booking flow, a product that needs explaining, or a way of serving customers that no page builder anticipated.

So we start with the business, not the layout. We look at how customers find you, what they need to decide, and what happens after they get in touch. Then we design the interface and build it properly — with clean components, sensible content models, and no mystery plugins waiting to break.

03Beliefs

What we
won't compromise.

These are not promises invented for a services page. They are the decisions that keep the work honest — and the reason the studio stays small.

01

The business comes first

We start with how you make money, serve customers, and run the day. The website follows the business — not the other way around.

02

Design is a decision

Every layout, label, and interaction should help someone decide or act. If it is only decoration, it has to earn its place.

03

Build it to be maintained

Clean components, readable code, and a content model you can live with. A website should not become a mystery after launch.

04

You own the thing

Your domain, your content, your accounts, your code. You should be able to leave with everything — and know how it works.
